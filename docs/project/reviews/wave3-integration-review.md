(5) The text was grepped against `terminology.md` and for British spellings.

## Remaining risks

- **Vendor defaults and prices** are the most perishable facts in Part IV: HPA tolerance (0.1), sync period (15 s) and stabilization window (5 min); ALB and HAProxy health-check defaults; Envoy's 10% ejection cap; Redis `io-threads`; Memcached segmented LRU "since 1.5.0"; illustrative CDN and egress prices; the Table 23.7 compressor benchmark. All carry "as of" tags or open verification items, but none were re-verified against primary sources here.
- **Running-example scale drift.** PhotoShare (Ch 1, 21, 22) and QuickBite (Ch 2, 18) now appear across many chapters. #3 and #7 show how easily a later chapter adopts its own numbers. An assumption register per running example (Table 2.10 does this for QuickBite) would make such drift visible.
- **Two capacity-sizing notations** (§18.11.2 and §23.10.3) are now linked but not unified. Chapter 89 (Capacity Estimation Handbook) should adopt one of them and cite both.
- **Reference drift.** About 510 references were again checked by a scratch script. `qa_check.py` still has no §-reference resolver, so renumbering in Ch 18–23, or in the TOC for Ch 24+, would break the 105 inbound references without warning.
